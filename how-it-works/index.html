How grabEAT works

Healthy Food 24/7

At grabEAT, we believe that healthy food doesn’t have to be a luxury. That’s why we bring nutritious, delicious, and convenient meals to you every day. Whether you’re looking for a quick meal during a busy day or want a healthier option on-the-go, our innovative kiosks have you covered.

For business partners

We help businesses provide their employees and customers with healthy, convenient, and innovative meal solutions right at their fingertips. Our smart kiosks are designed to offer freshly prepared meals and drinks, making it easier for businesses to enhance their workplace or service environment.

  1. 1

    Consultation & Customization

    Choose from our four kiosk options – MealPOD, MealPOD+, HUB or SmartHUB – based on your space and needs. These kiosks offer a range of healthy hot meals, drinks, and snacks.

  2. 2

    Seamless Installation & Setup

    Our team handles the delivery and installation, ensuring a quick and seamless setup. All you need is power and internet, and the grabEAT kiosk will be ready to serve your employees or customers.

  3. 3

    Maintenance & Refilling

    Our kiosks come with built-in software for real-time monitoring, tracking stock levels and sending alerts for restocking to avoid emptiness. Maintenance is done regularly by our staff to ensure everything runs smoothly.

For consumers

We make it easy for you to enjoy healthy, delicious meals and drinks — whenever you want. With our smart kiosks, you can quickly grab a nutritious meal without compromising on quality or taste.

  1. 1

    Find grabEAT Kiosk

    Our kiosks are conveniently located in offices, gyms, hospitals, and other public spaces. Whether you’re looking for a quick snack or a wholesome meal, grabEAT kiosk is always close by.

  2. 2

    Choose Your Meal

    Simply browse the menu on the touchscreen display. From warm, freshly prepared meals to cold & hot drinks and snacks, you can find a variety of nutritious options to satisfy your cravings.

  3. 3

    Pay Seamlessly

    grabEAT kiosks are fully cashless, allowing you to pay quickly and easily. Use your credit or debit card, mobile payment, or contactless options like Vipps, Apple Pay or Google Pay to complete your purchase.

  4. 4

    Enjoy Your Meal

    After you’ve made your selection, your meal is prepared in just a few minutes. You can then enjoy a nutritious, ready-to-eat dish, perfect for busy days or meals on-the-go.
