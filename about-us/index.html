About grabEAT

Our Journey Begins Here

At grabEAT, we’re passionate about making healthy, convenient meals accessible to everyone, no matter how busy life gets.

Founded in 2024, we are a startup with a simple mission: to bring nutritious, freshly-prepared food to people on the go—anytime, anywhere. Our automated kiosks and innovative solutions are designed to fit seamlessly into your daily routine, offering a range of delicious meals accessible 24/7.

Our Story

grabEAT started with a clear vision: to solve the everyday challenge of finding healthy food options that are both quickly and easily accessible. We saw the need for better choices—options that didn’t sacrifice quality or nutrition for speed. So, we set out to create a new kind of food experience, one that blends the ease of technology with the goodness of freshly-made meals.

Our Mission

We’re here to make healthy eating effortless and accessible for everyone. Whether you’re a busy professional, a student, or a traveler, we believe you deserve nutritious meals that fit your lifestyle. grabEAT is committed to offering a variety of balanced, high-quality meals that cater to different tastes and dietary needs.

What We Stand For

Innovation at Our Core

Our automated kiosks are designed to serve healthy, warm meals at the touch of a button. By harnessing technology, we’re creating a future where good food is always within reach.

Convenience Meets Quality

We’re more than just a quick bite. At grabEAT, we prioritize locally sourced fresh ingredients and carefully crafted recipes that give you the nutrition you need without compromising on flavour.

Sustainability & Social Impact

At grabEAT, we believe businesses should drive positive change. We are committed to sustainability by reducing food waste, sourcing responsibly, supporting local suppliers, and using eco-friendly packaging.

Healthy Choices for All

From vegan and vegetarian options to protein-packed meals and kid-friendly choices, our menu is built to support a variety of dietary preferences. No matter your needs, we’ve got something for you.

This is just a beginning...

We’re just getting started, but we have big ambitions. Our goal is to expand across Bergen City, and eventually reach communities nationwide and globally. We’re eager to grow, learn, and continue refining our service so that grabEAT can become a trusted solution for quickly and easily accessible healthy meals in busy environments.
